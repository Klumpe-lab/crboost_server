2026-11-19, roadmap 63 D7 (which images may be published: NVIDIA EULA, GPL source offers, Anaconda ToS,
ChimeraX licence): the maintainer waved it through as bureaucracy and accepted the rule without reading into it.

**Why:** the maintainer cares about the release working and shipping; licence mechanics are not where they want
to spend attention.
**How to apply:** when compliance applies (licences in images, notices, source offers, ToS), write it as a rule
inside the relevant stage and carry it out; only surface it as a question when it blocks something the maintainer
wants (e.g. a tool that cannot be published at all), in one line. Related [[project_public_release_roadmap63]].
